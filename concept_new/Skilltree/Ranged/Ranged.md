# Ranged

Stat node: [[Dexterity]] · Sub-trees: [[Bow]] · [[Crossbow]] · [[Trident]]

- **Bows:** arrows fly in an arc (gravity), and the interval between shots is the attack speed.
- **Crossbows:** bolts fly flatter, with a reload time between shots.
- **Tridents:** melee stab on LMB and throw on RMB; you have to get the trident back.
