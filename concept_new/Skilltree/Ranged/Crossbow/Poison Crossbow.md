# Poison Crossbow

Sub-tree: [[Crossbow]] · Slot type: **Attack**

*Old: "Damage over time (Stackable), Low Base dmg, Medium Range, Medium ATK Speed, Medium Pierce, Only first hit poisons"*

## Design
- As written: the bolt pierces several enemies, but **only the first enemy hit** gets [[Status Effects#Poison|Poison]].
- The first hit adds **2 stacks**, so it builds poison quickly on a single target.
- This is the counterpart to [[Poison Darts]], which spread single stacks over groups.
