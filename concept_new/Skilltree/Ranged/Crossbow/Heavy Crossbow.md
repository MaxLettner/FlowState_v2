# Heavy Crossbow

Sub-tree: [[Crossbow]] · Slot type: **Attack**

*Old: "Very low speed, Very High Damage, Infinite Pierce (Until wall), Flies Straight (No bullet drop)"*

## Design
- A **rail bolt**: flies perfectly straight with no drop and **∞ pierce** until it hits terrain.
- **Pin:** the last enemy hit before the bolt reaches a wall is [[Status Effects#Pin|pinned]] to that wall.
- Very slow reload.
