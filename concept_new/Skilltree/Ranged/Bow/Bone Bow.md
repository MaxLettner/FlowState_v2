# Bone Bow

Sub-tree: [[Bow]] · Slot type: **Attack**

*Old: "Medium Range, Medium Damage, Low Pierce"*

## Design
- A balanced bow with pierce 1.
- **Shattering arrows:** when an arrow stops (on its last enemy or on terrain), it splinters into **3 bone shards** that fly short distances in a cone and hit nearby enemies.

## Starting values
| Damage | Interval | Range | Pierce | Shards |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 15 (Medium) | 0.6 s | ~900 px (Medium) | 1 | 3 × 5 dmg, 120 px |
