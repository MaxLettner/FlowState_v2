# War Bow

Sub-tree: [[Bow]] · Slot type: **Attack** (hold to draw)

*Old: "High Damage, Low speed, High Range, High Pierce (3)"*

## Design
- **Hold LMB to draw** and release to fire. Movement is slowed while drawing.
- Damage, arrow speed and pierce scale with draw time. A full draw flies almost straight across the screen.
- Releasing below the minimum draw fires a weak arrow.

## Starting values
| Draw | Damage | Arrow speed | Pierce |
|---|---|---|---|
| Min (0.3 s) | 20 | 900 px/s | 1 |
| Full (1.5 s) | 60 (High) | 1800 px/s | 3 |
