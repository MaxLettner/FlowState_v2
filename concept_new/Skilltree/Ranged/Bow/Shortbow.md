# Shortbow

Sub-tree: [[Bow]] · Slot type: **Attack**

*Old: "High ATK speed, Short range, low dmg, No pierce"*

## Design
- As written: **fast click shots**, one arrow per click.
- Arrows lose speed quickly and despawn after a short distance, which makes it the close-range bow.
