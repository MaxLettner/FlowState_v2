# Dexterity (stat node)

*Old: "More base dmg for ranged attacks, satisfies requirements"*

- Repeatable, 1 SP per rank, max 10.
- Per rank: **+5 % ranged damage** (+50 % at max). This includes trident stabs.
- The rank count is the requirement for Ranged sub-trees and skills (see [[Progression]]).
