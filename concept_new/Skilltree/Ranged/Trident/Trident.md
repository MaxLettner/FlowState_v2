# Trident (sub-tree)

Skills: [[Recall Trident]] · [[Ice Trident]] · [[Heavy Trident]]

*Old: "Normal Trident doesnt come back, Works melee and ranged"*

## Basic weapon: Trident (Attack · RMB throw)
- **LMB: stab.** A medium-reach melee thrust.
- **RMB: throw.** Flies straight toward the cursor and **sticks** in the first terrain it hits.
- A thrown trident **doesn't come back**: walk over it to pick it up. Until then, LMB/RMB do nothing for this weapon (switch to another hotbar slot to fight).
- If the thrown trident falls behind the screen or into a pit, it returns to your hand after 3 s.

| Mode | Damage | Interval | Reach / range | Pierce |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Stab | 15 | 0.6 s | 120 px | — |
| Throw | 25 | — | until terrain | 1 |

## Passive: Reunion
- After picking up a thrown trident, your **next stab deals double damage**.
