# Heavy Trident

Sub-tree: [[Trident]] · Slot type: **Attack** · RMB: **Charged throw**

*Old: "Flies Straight, Infinite Pierce, Long windup to throw, High Damage, High Melee Dmg, Slow melee speed"*

## Design
- **Stab:** slow but hits hard.
- **Throw:** hold RMB to wind up. There is a **minimum windup** before it can be released at all. Holding longer increases damage and speed. Movement is slowed during the windup.
- Flies perfectly straight with **∞ pierce** until it hits terrain. Pick it up like the basic trident.
