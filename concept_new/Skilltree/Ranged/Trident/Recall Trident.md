# Recall Trident

Sub-tree: [[Trident]] · Slot type: **Attack** · RMB: **Throw / Recall**

*Old: "Medium Range Damage, Medium Close Damage, No pierce (Pierce Buff => 1 Pierce), comes back"*

## Design
- Stab and throw like the basic [[Trident]]. The throw has **no pierce** (+1 with [[Piercing]]).
- After throwing, **press RMB again to recall**: it flies back to your hand and **hits every enemy on the way back**.
- Recalling can be done at any time, even mid-flight. Positioning yourself so that enemies are between you and the trident is the main skill.
