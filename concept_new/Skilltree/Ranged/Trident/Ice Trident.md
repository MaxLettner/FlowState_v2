# Ice Trident

Sub-tree: [[Trident]] · Slot type: **Attack** · RMB: **Throw**

*Old: "Freezes Enemies with AOE attack on throw, Slows Down enemies on melee hit, Medium DMG, Medium Speed"*

## Design
- **Stab:** applies [[Status Effects#Slow|Slow]].
- **Throw:** where it lands (enemy or terrain), an **AOE freeze** [[Status Effects#Freeze|freezes]] all enemies in the radius.
- **Shatter:** frozen enemies take +100 % damage from the next hit of any kind, which removes the freeze (see [[Status Effects#Shatter|Shatter]]). Combos with [[Icicle]].
- Pick it up like the basic trident.

## Starting values
| Mode | Damage | Interval | Effect |
|---|---|---|---|
| Stab | 15 (Medium) | 0.6 s (Medium) | Slow 2 s |
| Throw | 20 | — | Freeze 1.5 s, radius 150 px |
