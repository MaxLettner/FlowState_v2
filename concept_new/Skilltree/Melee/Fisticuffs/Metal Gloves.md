# Metal Gloves

Sub-tree: [[Fisticuffs]] · Slot type: **Attack** · RMB: **Block**

*Old: "Medium Speed, Medium Damage, Low Range, 12% crit"*

## Design
- Balanced punches.
- **Hold RMB to block:** melee damage from the front is reduced by 60 %, and enemy projectiles (e.g. thrown spears) that hit the fists are **destroyed**. Movement is slowed and you can't attack while blocking.

## Starting values
| Damage | Interval | Reach | Crit | Block |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 12 (Low–Medium) | 0.4 s (Medium) | 50 px (Low) | 12 % | −60 % front melee dmg, destroys projectiles |
