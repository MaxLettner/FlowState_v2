# Leather Gloves

Sub-tree: [[Fisticuffs]] · Slot type: **Attack** · RMB: **Dodge**

*Old: "High Speed, Low Damage, 10% Crit"*

## Design
- The fastest melee weapon: rapid jabs that fill the [[Fisticuffs|Combo Meter]] quickly.
- **RMB: dodge dash.** A short dash in the movement direction with brief invulnerability.
