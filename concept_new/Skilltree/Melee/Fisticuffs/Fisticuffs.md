# Fisticuffs (sub-tree)

Skills: [[Leather Gloves]] · [[Metal Gloves]] · [[Spike Gloves]]

*Old: "Stuns on Crit and double damage"*

## Basic weapon: Hand Wraps (Attack)
- Low damage (8), high speed (0.3 s), reach 50 px, 8 % crit.

## Passive: Combo Meter
- Every glove hit within **1.5 s** of the previous one adds **+2 % crit chance** (max **+20 %**). The meter resets after 1.5 s without a hit.
- Glove **crits deal ×2 damage and [[Status Effects#Stun|stun]] for 0.5 s**.
- The HUD shows the meter as a small bar under the hotbar.
