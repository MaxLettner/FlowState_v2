# Morningstar

Sub-tree: [[Blunt]] · Slot type: **Attack** (hold to spin)

*Old: "High Range, Knockback, High Knockback, Slow attack Speed, 1% crit chance"*

## Design
- **Tap:** a slow, very long-reach swing with high knockback.
- **Hold: flail spin.** The ball spins around the player and hits everything in a circle repeatedly with knockback. Movement is halved while spinning. The spin lasts up to 3 s, then there is a 1 s recovery.
- This sets it apart from the [[Hammer]] (single target and stun) as the crowd-clearing blunt weapon.
