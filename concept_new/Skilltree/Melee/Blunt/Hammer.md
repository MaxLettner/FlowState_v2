# Hammer

Sub-tree: [[Blunt]] · Slot type: **Attack**

*Old: "Big 2 Hand Hammer, Medium Range, Slow attack Speed, Medium Dmg, 1% crit, stuns on hit, High Knockback"*

## Design
- **Ground attack:** a slow two-handed swing that **stuns** and knocks back.
- **Mid-air attack: ground slam.** The player drops fast, and on landing a **shockwave** stuns and damages every enemy in a radius.
