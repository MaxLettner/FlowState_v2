# Spring Hammer

Sub-tree: [[Blunt]] · Slot type: **Attack**

*Old: "High atk speed, Very low dmg, Very high knockback"*

## Design
- A crowd-control tool: fast taps with huge knockback and almost no damage.
- **Bounce:** an enemy launched by the Spring Hammer that crashes into another enemy deals collision damage to **both**.
- An enemy knocked into a **pit** dies (and counts as a kill for XP and [[Life Steal]]).
