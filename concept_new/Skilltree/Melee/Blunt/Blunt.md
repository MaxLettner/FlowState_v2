# Blunt (sub-tree)

Skills: [[Hammer]] · [[Morningstar]] · [[Spring Hammer]]

## Basic weapon: Club (Attack)
- Medium damage (15), low speed (0.9 s), reach 90 px, short stun (0.5 s), medium knockback.

## Passive: Concussion
- Stuns from Blunt weapons last **+30 %**, and stunned enemies take **+15 %** damage from Blunt weapons.
