# Dual Wielding

Sub-tree: [[Swords]] · Slot type: **Attack**

*Old: "2 katanas, High speed, Medium range, low dmg, 1% crit chance"*

## Design
- Two katanas that **alternate**: left blade, right blade, left…
- Very short interval, so **two swings can be in the air at once**.
- Low damage per hit, highest sword DPS, and the most hits (good with the [[Swords]] bleed passive and [[Imbue]]).

## Starting values
| Damage | Interval | Reach | Crit |
|---|---|---|---|
| 8 (Low) | 0.25 s (High speed) | 100 px (Medium) | 1 % |
