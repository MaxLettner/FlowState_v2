# Shortsword

Sub-tree: [[Swords]] · Slot type: **Attack**

*Old: "Short Attack Range, Medium DMG, Medium Speed, 3%"*

## Design
- **3-hit combo:** slash → slash → **finisher**.
- The combo continues if the next attack comes within 0.8 s, otherwise it resets.
- The finisher deals extra damage and knockback.

## Starting values
| Hit | Damage | Interval | Reach | Crit | Knockback |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 & 2 | 15 (Medium) | 0.45 s | 85 px (short) | 3 % | light |
| 3 (finisher) | 30 | 0.6 s | 85 px | 3 % | strong (450) |
