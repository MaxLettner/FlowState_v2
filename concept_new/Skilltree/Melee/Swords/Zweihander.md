# Zweihander

Sub-tree: [[Swords]] · Slot type: **Attack** (hold to charge)

*Old: "Low atk speed, High attack dmg, High attack range"*

## Design
- **Tap:** a slow, wide, long-reach swing.
- **Hold:** charge (move slower while charging). Releasing does a **heavy cleave** that hits *every* enemy in a large arc with high damage and strong knockback.
