# Levitation

Sub-tree: [[Arcane]] · Slot type: **Instant**

*Old: "Flying with continuous mana use, no initial mana use"*

## Design
- **Timed burst** instead of permanent flight, so pits and platforming still matter.
- Activation costs mana, then the player flies freely for a few seconds: **W** up, **S** down, nothing pressed = hover.
- When the time runs out the player falls slowly for 1 s (no sudden drop), then the cooldown starts.

## Starting values
| Mana | Flight time | Cooldown | Flight speed |
|---|---|---|---|
| 25 | 3 s | 10 s | 300 px/s |
