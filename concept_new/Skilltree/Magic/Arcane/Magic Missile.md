# Magic Missile

Sub-tree: [[Arcane]] · Slot type: **Attack**

*Old: "Homing Magic Missile, low DMG"*

## Design
- Fires toward the cursor. After a short distance it **homes in on the enemy nearest to the cursor** (within 250 px of the cursor).
- If no enemy is near the cursor, it keeps flying straight.
- Cheap and spammable; aiming still matters for picking the target.

## Starting values
| Mana | Damage | Interval | Pierce | Turn rate |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 8 | Low (10) | 0.3 s | 0 | 360°/s |
