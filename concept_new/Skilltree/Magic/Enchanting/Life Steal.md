# Life Steal

Sub-tree: [[Enchanting]] · Slot type: **Toggle**

*Old: "Steals Health based on outputted DMG"*

## Design
- While active, **every kill heals** a share of your max HP, no matter how the enemy died (weapon, spell, DOT, knockback into a pit).
- This rewards finishing enemies. It works the same for every build.

## Starting values
| Drain | Heal per kill |
|---|---|
| 2 mana/s | 8 % max HP |
