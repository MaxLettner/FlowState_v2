# Piercing

Sub-tree: [[Enchanting]] · Slot type: **Toggle**

*Old: "Adds Pierce to projectiles"*

## Design
- While active, every player projectile gets **+2 pierce**: arrows, bolts, spells and thrown tridents.
- Exception: the [[Recall Trident]] gets only **+1** (as its original note says).
- Projectiles that already have ∞ pierce are unchanged.

## Starting values
| Drain | Bonus pierce |
|---|---|
| 2 mana/s | +2 (Recall Trident +1) |
