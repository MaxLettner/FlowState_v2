# Enchanting (sub-tree)

Skills: [[Imbue]] · [[Life Steal]] · [[Piercing]]

All enchantments are **Toggles** that drain mana per second. Only **one enchantment** can be active at a time.

## Basic enchantment: Sharpen (Toggle)
- Weapon hits (melee and ranged, not spells) deal **+15 % damage**. Drain 1.5 mana/s.

## Passive: Efficient Enchanting
- All enchantment mana drain is reduced by **25 %**.
