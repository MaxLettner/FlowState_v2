# Icicle

Sub-tree: [[Elemental]] · Slot type: **Attack**

*Old: "Medium Mana cost, shoots icecle"*

## Design
- A fast ice shard. Each hit applies [[Status Effects#Slow|Slow]].
- Hitting an enemy that is **already Slowed or Frozen** triggers [[Status Effects#Shatter|Shatter]] for bonus damage and removes the status.
- Combos with the [[Ice Trident]] and the Ice option of [[Imbue]].
