# Fire Ball

Sub-tree: [[Elemental]] · Slot type: **Attack** (hold to charge)

*Old: "Straight flying missile, AOE Damage, Damage over time, High Damage, High Mana cost"*

## Design
- **Hold LMB to charge** and release to fire. Flies straight.
- Explodes on the first enemy or terrain hit (AOE).
- Leaves a **burning ground** patch. Enemies standing in it get [[Status Effects#Burn|Burn]].
- **Longer charge** gives more explosion damage, a bigger radius, a longer-lasting fire patch and a higher mana cost. Mana is paid on release.
