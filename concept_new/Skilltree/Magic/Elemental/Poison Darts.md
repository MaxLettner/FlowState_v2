# Poison Darts

Sub-tree: [[Elemental]] · Slot type: **Attack**

*Old: "Stackable DMG over time, Low mana cost"*

## Design
- Fires **3 darts in a small fan** (±5°).
- Each dart that hits adds **1 [[Status Effects#Poison|Poison]] stack**. At close range all three hit one enemy (3 stacks); at long range they spread across a group.
- Low direct damage; the stacks do the work.

|---|---|---|---|
