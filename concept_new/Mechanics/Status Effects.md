# Status Effects

| Effect | Behaviour | Stacking | Sources |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Stun** | Enemy cannot move or attack | Longest duration wins | [[Hammer]], [[Fisticuffs]] crits, [[Blunt]] passive |
| **Knockback** | Pushes the enemy away with a small hop; no AI while flying | — | Blunt weapons, finishers, [[Zweihander]] |
| **Slow** | −40 % movement speed and attack speed | Refreshes, doesn't stack | [[Icicle]], [[Ice Trident]] stab, [[Imbue]] (Ice) |
| **Freeze** | Full stop, like a stun; the enemy is tinted blue | Refreshes | [[Ice Trident]] throw |
| **Shatter** | Hitting a **Frozen** enemy with any hit removes the freeze and deals **+100 %** of that hit. Hitting a **Slowed** enemy with an [[Icicle]] removes the slow and deals **+15** bonus damage | Consumes the status | Ice skills |
| **Burn** | 5 dmg/s for 3 s | Refreshes, doesn't stack | [[Fire Ball]] burning ground, [[Imbue]] (Fire) |
| **Poison** | 2 dmg/s **per stack**, 5 s, a new stack refreshes the duration | Up to 10 stacks | [[Poison Darts]], [[Poison Crossbow]], [[Imbue]] (Poison) |
| **Bleed** | 3 dmg/s **per stack**, 4 s | Up to 5 stacks | [[Spike Gloves]], [[Swords]] passive |
| **Pin** | Enemy is stuck to a wall (counts as a stun) | — | [[Heavy Crossbow]] |

**Pierce N** means the projectile passes through N enemies, so it hits N + 1 in total. **∞** means it only stops at terrain.
