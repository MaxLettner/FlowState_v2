# Progression

## Earning skill points (SP)
- Level up: +1 SP (XP from kills; the XP needed per level grows by 1 each level).
- Chests: +1 SP.

## Stat ranks
Each branch has one repeatable stat node: [[Strength]], [[Dexterity]] or [[Mana]].
- Cost: **1 SP per rank**, max **10 ranks**.
- The first rank opens the branch.

## Sub-tree nodes
- Cost: **2 SP**. Requires **rank 1** of the branch stat.
- Unlocking grants the sub-tree's **basic weapon/spell** and its **passive**.

## Skills (leaves)
All three skills of a sub-tree can be unlocked. Each additional skill in the **same sub-tree** costs more and needs more ranks:

| Skill # in this sub-tree | SP cost | Required stat rank |
|---|---|---|
| 1st | 3 | 3 |
| 2nd | 4 | 5 |
| 3rd | 5 | 7 |

The order is free: whichever skill you buy first is "1st".

## Total cost of a full tree
- Stat ranks: 3 × 10 = 30
- Sub-trees: 9 × 2 = 18
- Skills: 9 × (3 + 4 + 5) = 108
- **Total: 156 SP**
