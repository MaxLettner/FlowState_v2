# Tiers

The skill notes use qualitative tiers like the old concept. These are the proposed **starting values** for balancing (melee enemy HP = 100).

| Tier | Damage per hit | Attack interval | Range (melee reach / projectile range) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Very Low | 1–5 | ≥ 1.2 s ("Very low speed") | — |
| Low | 6–12 | 0.8–1.0 s ("Low speed") | 50 px / ~500 px |
| Medium | 13–25 | 0.5–0.7 s | 85–110 px / ~900 px |
| High | 30–45 | 0.25–0.4 s ("High speed") | 140–160 px / ~1400 px |
| Very High | 50+ | ≤ 0.2 s | screen-wide |

- Default crit chance is 1–3 %. A crit deals **×2** damage (Fisticuffs crits also stun).
- Mana: 100 base, +10 per [[Mana]] rank, regenerates 5 %/s while no Toggle drains mana.
